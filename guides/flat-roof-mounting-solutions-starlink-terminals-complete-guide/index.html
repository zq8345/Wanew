1. Why Choose a Flat Roof Mount

Flat roofs are one of the most common installation surfaces for Starlink terminals, particularly for commercial buildings, apartments, and RVs. A properly installed flat roof mount provides:

  • Optimal sky view — Fewer obstructions compared to ground-level installation
  • Security — Dish is out of reach, theft-resistant
  • Clean aesthetics — No ground cables to trip over
  • Signal quality — Higher elevation reduces interference from ground-level structures

4. Installation by Roof Type

TPO / EPDM / PVC Membrane Roofs (Commercial)

  1. Locate structural supports (joists/trusses) using blueprints or a stud finder
  2. Mark bolt positions and apply urethane sealant to each bolt hole
  3. Use rubber gaskets under the base plate to distribute load
  4. Bolt through the membrane into structural supports
  5. Apply a cover patch over the bolt heads using compatible membrane bonding adhesive
  6. Seal edges with roofing-grade sealant — not standard silicone

Critical: Do NOT penetrate the roof membrane without proper sealing. A single unsealed bolt hole can cause leaks that damage insulation and interior ceilings. Always use the included rubber compression gaskets.

Modified Bitumen Roofs

Modified bitumen roofs require a different approach. The material is thicker and more forgiving for penetrations, but still requires proper sealing:

  1. Cut a small cross in the bitumen surface at bolt locations
  2. Apply cold-applied bitumen adhesive around each penetration
  3. Install bolts through the base plate
  4. Apply additional bitumen patch over bolt heads
  5. Allow 24 hours curing before applying wind load

Metal Roofs

Metal roofs are the easiest flat roof type for mounting. Use standing seam clamps instead of drilling where possible:

  • Standing seam clamp method — No roof penetration needed. Attach a 2 x 4 wood or aluminum base to the clamps, then mount the bracket on the base. Best for metal roofs with raised seams.
  • Drilling method — Use self-tapping sheet metal screws with neoprene washers. Seal screws with polyurethane sealant.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a flat roof mount without drilling?

A: Yes, for metal roofs with standing seams, use seam clamps. For membrane roofs, weighted (ballasted) mounting systems are available but require approval from a structural engineer for roof load capacity.

How much weight can a flat roof mount hold?

A: Our standard mounts are rated for up to 15 kg static load. The roof itself must be able to support the combined weight of the mount and terminal (typically 3-8 kg total) plus snow load in winter climates.

Will the mount damage the roof membrane over time?

A: With proper installation using rubber gaskets and sealant, the mount should not damage the membrane. We recommend annual inspection of the sealant condition. UV degradation of sealant occurs in 3-5 years and reapplication may be needed.
