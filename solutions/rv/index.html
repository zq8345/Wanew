SOLUTIONS · RV & OVERLAND

Stay connected on every road

Vibration-ready mounts, 12 V/DC power, and the compact Starlink Mini keep RVs, vans and overland rigs online from the trailhead to the campsite.

What makes an RV different

01

You drive with it

Road vibration works fasteners loose.

02

There is no wall socket

Power comes off the 12V or 24V system.

03

You set up and pack down

Mounts have to come off without tools.

04

Roof space is crowded

Racks, vents and AC leave little room.

What an RV setup needs

A mount that survives the road

Magnetic, clamp or ladder, matched to the surface.

12V power, no inverter

A DC adapter instead of mains through a converter.

Cable that tolerates movement

Weather-rated runs from roof to cabin.

Quick to remove

So the dish is not left up at highway speed.
