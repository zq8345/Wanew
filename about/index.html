About Us

Engineering-led manufacturing since 2010.

Wanew Starlink Accessories is an engineering-led manufacturer based in the Guangdong–Hong Kong–Macao Greater Bay Area, running R&D, production and sales under one roof — a one-stop source for the full range of Starlink-terminal peripherals. Every product is engineered to meet the weather-resistance and outdoor demands of Starlink Mini, Standard, and Performance (Gen 1/2/3) terminals, giving global buyers a stable, durable, and precisely fitted one-stop supply.

Compatibility & FAQ

Wanew is an independent third-party manufacturer and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by SpaceX or Starlink. “Starlink” is a trademark of SpaceX; references here indicate compatibility only.

Are Wanew products official Starlink accessories?

No. They are independently designed and tested for compatibility.

Will using Wanew void my Starlink warranty?

Third-party accessories do not automatically void warranty. Damage caused by accessories may not be covered.

Does SpaceX recommend Wanew?

SpaceX does not endorse any third-party accessory manufacturer.
