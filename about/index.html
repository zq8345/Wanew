About Us

Engineering-led manufacturing since 2010.

Wanew Starlink Accessories is an engineering-led manufacturer of Starlink-compatible accessories, based in the Guangdong–Hong Kong–Macao Greater Bay Area. Under one integrated R&D, production and sales operation, we design and build a full range of peripherals for Starlink terminals — waterproof network and power cables, mounts and brackets, power adapters, protective cases, and signal-expansion hardware.

Every product is engineered to meet the weather-resistance and outdoor demands of Starlink Mini, Standard, and Performance (Gen 1/2/3) terminals, giving global buyers a stable, durable, and precisely fitted one-stop supply. Wanew is an independent, third-party manufacturer and is not affiliated with SpaceX or Starlink.

FAQ

Are Wanew products official Starlink accessories?

No. They are independently designed and tested for compatibility.

Will using Wanew void my Starlink warranty?

Third-party accessories do not automatically void warranty. Damage caused by accessories may not be covered.

Does SpaceX recommend Wanew?

SpaceX does not endorse any third-party accessory manufacturer.
