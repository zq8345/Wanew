1. Mount Types at a Glance

Wanew offers three main types of mounts, each designed for a specific installation scenario. Choosing the right one depends on where you plan to install your Starlink terminal, whether you own or rent your property, and your tolerance for installation complexity.

2. Universal Pipe Mount

Key Features

  • Clamps onto existing pipes, railings, or poles (1-3 inch diameter)
  • No drilling or permanent modification required
  • Stainless steel bracket with powder-coated finish
  • Includes adapter plates for all Starlink terminal models
  • Rubber padding protects the mounting surface

Ideal Use Cases

SituationWhy Pipe Mount Works
RV with a ladderMounts on the RV ladder in 10 minutes; no roof modification
Apartment balconyClamps onto balcony railing; removable when moving out
Deck or porchAttaches to a deck post or pergola beam
Ground poleMount on a pre-existing pole in the yard
Temporary/camping setupCan be moved between different locations easily

Limitations

  • Requires a suitable pipe or pole within cable reach of the power source
  • Pipe must be structurally sound (check for wobble before mounting)
  • Less discreet than a wall mount (visible bracket and cables)
  • Not suitable for Performance terminals in high-wind areas without reinforcement

3. Wall Mount

Key Features

  • Low-profile design that mounts flush against an exterior wall
  • Fixed or articulating arm options available
  • Works with wood, brick, masonry, and stucco walls
  • Clean cable routing through the wall into the building interior
  • No roof penetration needed

Ideal Use Cases

SituationWhy Wall Mount Works
Homeowner with a clear south-facing wallClean, permanent installation with minimal visual impact
Single-story houseEasy access for installation and maintenance
Below-eave installationArticulating arm extends past the eave for clear sky view
Renter with owner permissionMinimal wall damage (2-4 bolt holes easily patched)

Limitations

  • Limited to the hemisphere facing the wall (may restrict sky view)
  • Not suitable if the wall faces north (in the northern hemisphere)
  • Requires drilling into the wall (except with the no-drill adapter kit)
  • Less suitable for Standard Actuated terminals (motor movement may hit the wall)

4. Flat Roof Mount

Key Features

  • Elevated mounting post (6-24 inches) for clear sky view above roofline
  • Available in low-profile, adjustable, and heavy-duty versions
  • Compatible with TPO, EPDM, PVC, modified bitumen, and metal roofs
  • Multiple height and tilt angle adjustments for optimal positioning
  • Highest wind rating of all mount types (up to 120 mph for heavy-duty)

Ideal Use Cases

7. Real-World Scenarios

Scenario A: Full-Time RVer

Situation: A couple lives in their RV full-time and needs reliable Starlink internet. They move between campgrounds every 2-4 weeks.

Recommendation: Universal Pipe Mount. Clamps onto the RV ladder or roof rack in 10 minutes. Can be easily removed and reinstalled at each new location without leaving permanent marks.

Scenario B: Homeowner with a Flat Roof

Situation: A family in a single-story house with a flat TPO roof needs Starlink for work-from-home and streaming. They own the property and want the best possible connection.

Recommendation: Flat Roof Mount. The elevated position ensures the best sky view. Use the adjustable version to fine-tune the angle seasonally. Total cost: approximately $79 for the mount + professional installation if needed.

Scenario C: Apartment Renter

Situation: A university student rents a second-floor apartment and wants Starlink Mini for study and gaming. Cannot drill into the walls or roof.

Recommendation: Universal Pipe Mount on the balcony railing, or a Window Sill Mount if no railing is available. Both options require no permanent modification and can be removed when moving out.

Scenario D: Commercial Building

Situation: A warehouse manager needs reliable connectivity for inventory systems and office operations. The building has a large flat membrane roof.

Recommendation: Flat Roof Mount (heavy-duty version). The reinforced bracket and larger base plate provide stability for the Performance terminal that the warehouse needs for reliable uptime. DDP shipping available through Wanew's US warehouse.

8. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch between mount types later?

A: Yes. Wanew mounts use standardized bolt patterns for each terminal model. You can unscrew the terminal from one mount and reattach it to a different mount type in minutes. For example, start with a Pipe Mount for immediate setup, then switch to a Roof Mount for a permanent installation later.

Which mount has the easiest installation?

A: The Universal Pipe Mount is the easiest — no drilling, no special tools, and it installs in 10-15 minutes. The Wall Mount is next easiest (requires drilling into a wall). The Flat Roof Mount is the most complex and may require professional help.

Do I need different mounts for different Starlink terminals?

A: The mount bracket is universal, but the adapter plate is terminal-specific. Wanew includes the correct adapter plate for your terminal model with each mount purchase. If you upgrade your terminal later, you can order a new adapter plate instead of buying a whole new mount.
