5. Ordering Process

  1. Inquiry — Submit your requirements via email (product type, quantity, target price, delivery timeline)
  2. Quotation — We respond within 24 hours with a formal quotation, including MOQ, lead time, and payment terms
  3. Sample Approval — For first orders, samples are shipped within 3-5 days for quality evaluation
  4. Order Confirmation — Proforma invoice issued. 30% deposit required (T/T) or L/C at sight
  5. Production — Production begins after deposit receipt. QC inspection at key milestones
  6. Final Inspection — Third-party inspection available (SGS, Bureau Veritas) at buyer's cost
  7. Shipping — Balance payment before shipment. DDP to US warehouse or FOB Shenzhen
  8. After-Sales — 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Replacement or credit for defective units

7. Frequently Asked Questions

What payment methods do you accept?

A: T/T (bank wire transfer) and L/C (letter of credit) are our standard payment methods. For established partners, we can discuss net 30/60 terms after 6 months of consistent ordering history.

Do you offer white-label / private label packaging?

A: Yes. We offer full private label services including custom packaging, branding on products, and instruction manuals. Minimum order for private label: 1,000 units per SKU. Design setup fee: $200-$500 per SKU.

What is your quality guarantee?

A: All products carry a 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Our standard defect rate is below 0.5% (verified by third-party audits). Defective units are replaced or credited within 15 days of notification.

Do you have a US warehouse?

A: Yes. We maintain inventory at our US warehouse for fast delivery to North American customers. DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) terms available — no customs clearance required on your end.
